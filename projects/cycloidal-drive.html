Miniature Cycloidal Drive

Overview

Designed and built a small-scale cycloidal drive using Fusion 360, PLA prints, and AliExpress ball bearings. The goal is a compact, high-ratio gearbox for robotics applications without relying on off-the-shelf planetary stages.

What I did

  • Modeled the cycloidal profile and housing in Fusion 360
  • Printed prototypes in PLA and iterated on tolerances
  • Sourced and fitted ball bearings for the eccentric cam and output stage
  • Bench testing still in progress — torque and backlash measurements to come
